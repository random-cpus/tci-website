OUR STORY

Built by operators, for the long term.

TCI was founded with a single conviction: that the most valuable companies of the next decade will be built at the intersection of technology, capital and operating excellence.

We partner with exceptional founders and operators to construct enduring companies — providing not just capital, but the infrastructure, talent and distribution that accelerates breakout growth.

Our model combines operating discipline with permanent capital, designed to compound value across cycles rather than chase exits.

OPERATING PRINCIPLES

How we build value.

Long-term capital

We invest with a multi-decade horizon, free from quarterly pressure — backing companies built to compound over generations, not exit cycles.

Operator-led

Every company we own is backed by hands-on operating expertise, not just capital. Our partners build alongside founders.

Frontier categories

We focus on industries being rewritten by AI, data and digital infrastructure — where the next decade of value will be created.

Compound advantage

Our portfolio companies share talent, technology and distribution — creating advantages no single venture could build alone.
